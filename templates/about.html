PROJECT TITLE

AI-Based Startup Idea Validation Assistant

The application captures a founder's assumptions, converts self-assessed factors into an explainable score, and produces a SWOT review, MVP outline, and validation roadmap.

PythonFlaskSQLiteHTML / CSSJavaScript
⌕

Problem statement

Early-stage founders may invest in building a solution before confirming customer needs, differentiation, feasibility, or willingness to pay. The system brings these assumptions into one structured review.

◎

Project objectives

  • Evaluate key business assumptions consistently.
  • Identify strengths, gaps, and open evidence questions.
  • Recommend low-cost MVP and validation experiments.
  • Save reports locally for review and iteration.
⌘

Assessment engine

The default mode uses a transparent weighted rule-based engine, with optional AI-assisted narrative enhancement if configured. It runs without an API key.

Scoring methodology

Eight founder-rated factors are mapped from a 1–5 rating onto a 0–100 factor score.

⚑

Limitations

  • The assessment is only as reliable as the information entered.
  • It does not conduct automatic market research or verify competitors.
  • It cannot confirm product-market fit or guarantee business success.
  • AI enhancement, if enabled, may be incomplete or incorrect and must be reviewed.

Future enhancements
